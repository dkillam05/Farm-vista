FarmVista Platform

One system for the work already happening across the farm.

FarmVista brings multiple areas of the operation together without trying to turn farming into an office-only process. Information can be entered where the work happens and used throughout the rest of the system.

🌾

Grain Management

Track grain tickets, buyers, destinations, contracts, customer assignments, grade information and contract reconciliation.

🚛

Load-Out & Logistics

Dispatch loads, assign drivers, track destinations and grain sources, manage preloads and connect scanned grain tickets back to the load.

🏢

Grain Inventory

Track grain stored in bins and grain bags, record movements and keep inventory connected to actual grain activity.

🌦️

Field Readiness

Combine recent conditions, rainfall and forecast information into a practical field-readiness view for day-to-day planning.

🚜

Equipment & Maintenance

Keep equipment information, repair requests, service activity and maintenance workflows available to the people who need them.

👥

Employees & Permissions

Manage employees, roles and permission groups so each person gets the FarmVista tools appropriate for their responsibilities.

🧾

Grain Ticket Scanning

Capture grain tickets from a phone, extract ticket information and connect the ticket into FarmVista's grain workflow.

✅

Pre-Trip Inspections

Drivers can complete pre-trip inspections while office staff can quickly see submissions and equipment defects.

🔔

Alerts & Notifications

FarmVista can surface important operational information and send configured grain quality alerts to enrolled users.

Connected Operations

Data entered once can keep moving with the job.

The goal is not another place to type information. FarmVista is being built so the details collected during one part of the job can support the next part.

✓
Load-out information can follow the load into the grain ticket workflow.
✓
Grain tickets can connect back to contracts and inventory.
✓
Employee permissions control what tools and actions are available.
✓
The dashboard brings key farm information together without requiring users to open every module.
