How These Alerts Work

Severe: a single ticket at or above the severe limit can trigger an alert.
Repeated Elevated: several consecutive tickets at or above the elevated limit can trigger a pattern alert, even if none of those tickets reaches the severe limit.
A backend alert function will use these saved settings when we connect SMS delivery.
