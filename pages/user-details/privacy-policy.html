FarmVista Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 17, 2026

FarmVista is an agricultural operations application used to manage farm, field, logistics, grain, equipment, employee, and related operational information. This Privacy Policy explains what information FarmVista may collect, how that information is used, and how mobile messaging information is handled.

Information We Collect

FarmVista may collect information that users provide directly or that is created through use of the application, including:

  • Name, email address, mobile phone number, role, and other account or profile information.
  • Farm, field, crop, grain, contract, load, ticket, equipment, logistics, and operational records.
  • Photos, documents, notes, reports, and other information uploaded to FarmVista.
  • Technical and usage information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.
  • Notification preferences and information needed to send operational notifications.

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide and operate FarmVista, maintain records, coordinate agricultural and logistics work, send requested or operational notifications, support users, protect the service, and improve application functionality.

SMS and Mobile Messaging

Users who provide a mobile phone number and consent to receive text messages may receive FarmVista operational SMS messages. These messages may include grain load assignments, delivery information, grain-ticket instructions, status notifications, or other FarmVista operational information.

Message frequency varies based on FarmVista activity and assigned work. Message and data rates may apply. Users may reply STOP to opt out of SMS messages and HELP for help.

Mobile Information and Marketing

FarmVista does not sell or share mobile phone numbers, SMS consent, or SMS opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information is used only as necessary to provide FarmVista services and deliver requested or operational communications, including through service providers that support message delivery.

Service Providers

FarmVista may use trusted service providers to host the application, store data, authenticate users, deliver notifications, process communications, and support application operations. These providers may process information only as needed to provide their services to FarmVista.

Data Security

FarmVista uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards intended to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate FarmVista, maintain agricultural and business records, meet operational needs, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with applicable requirements.

Your Choices

Users may update information available through their FarmVista profile and may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. Certain operational records may need to be retained for business, accounting, safety, or recordkeeping purposes.

Children's Privacy

FarmVista is an agricultural operations application and is not intended for children under 13. FarmVista does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date shown above will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or FarmVista privacy practices may be directed through the FarmVista application administrator.
