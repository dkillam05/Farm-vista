FarmVista Privacy Policy

FarmVista is an agricultural operations application used to manage farm, field, logistics, grain, equipment, employee, and related operational information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information FarmVista may collect, how that information is used, and how mobile messaging information is handled.

Information We Collect

FarmVista may collect information that users provide directly or that is created through use of the application, including:

  • Name, email address, mobile phone number, role, and other account or profile information.
  • Farm, field, crop, grain, contract, load, ticket, equipment, logistics, and operational records.
  • Photos, documents, notes, reports, and other information uploaded to FarmVista.
  • Technical and usage information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.
  • Notification preferences and information needed to send operational notifications.

How We Use Information

Information collected by FarmVista is used to operate and provide the FarmVista application and its features.

This may include maintaining agricultural and business records, coordinating field and logistics work, managing grain loads and tickets, assigning work, delivering operational notifications, supporting users, protecting the application, troubleshooting problems, and improving FarmVista functionality.

SMS and Mobile Messaging

Users who provide a mobile phone number and consent to receive text messages may receive FarmVista operational SMS messages.

Messages may include grain load assignments, delivery destinations, crop information, grain ticket instructions, links used to submit or scan grain tickets, or other FarmVista operational information.

Message frequency varies based on FarmVista activity and the number of loads, jobs, or other work assigned to the user.

Message and data rates may apply.

SMS Opt-Out: Users may reply STOP to stop receiving SMS messages. Users may reply HELP for messaging assistance.

Mobile Information and Marketing

FarmVista does not sell or share mobile phone numbers, SMS consent, or SMS opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile information is used only as necessary to provide FarmVista services and deliver requested or operational communications.

FarmVista may use service providers that assist with communication delivery, hosting, authentication, data storage, or other services required to operate the application.

Service Providers

FarmVista may use trusted third-party service providers to host the application, store information, authenticate users, deliver messages or notifications, and support other technical functions required by FarmVista.

These providers may process information only as needed to provide their services to FarmVista.

Information Sharing

FarmVista does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to provide FarmVista services, comply with legal requirements, protect FarmVista or its users, prevent fraud or misuse, or support the legitimate operation of the application.

SMS consent and mobile opt-in information are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes.

Data Security

FarmVista uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards intended to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure.

No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate FarmVista, maintain agricultural and business records, support application functionality, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet applicable legal or operational requirements.

Your Choices

Users may update certain profile information through FarmVista.

Users may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP.

Certain agricultural, accounting, logistics, safety, audit, or other operational records may need to be retained even after a user stops using FarmVista.

Children's Privacy

FarmVista is an agricultural operations application and is not intended for children under 13.

FarmVista does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

FarmVista may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as application features, services, or legal requirements change.

The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy is revised.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy, FarmVista privacy practices, or FarmVista messaging may be directed to the FarmVista application administrator.
