SMS Messaging

Effective Date: August 19, 2026

FarmVista SMS Messaging Program

FarmVista uses SMS text messaging to provide agricultural and operational communications to users who provide a mobile phone number and affirmatively consent to receive FarmVista text messages.

FarmVista SMS messages are used for operational communications and are not used for third-party marketing or promotional advertising.

How Users Opt In

A FarmVista user may provide a mobile phone number through FarmVista and choose to receive operational SMS messages.

SMS messaging is optional. Providing a mobile phone number alone does not constitute consent to receive FarmVista SMS messages.

Affirmative consent is required.

The user must affirmatively agree to receive FarmVista operational text messages. The SMS consent option is not selected by default.

By opting in, the user agrees to receive operational SMS messages from FarmVista at the mobile number provided.

Types of Messages

FarmVista SMS messages may include:

  • Grain load assignments.
  • Load numbers and crop information.
  • Buyer, customer, or delivery destination information.
  • Grain ticket submission or scanning instructions.
  • Links associated with assigned FarmVista work.
  • Other FarmVista agricultural or operational notifications requested by or relevant to the user.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies.

The number of messages received depends on the number of FarmVista loads, assignments, jobs, alerts, or other operational activities associated with the user.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on the user's wireless carrier and mobile plan.

Consent Is Optional

Consent to receive FarmVista SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing goods or services.

Users who do not consent to SMS messaging may continue to use FarmVista features available to their account, subject to their assigned access and permissions.

Opt Out

To stop receiving FarmVista SMS messages at any time, reply STOP to a FarmVista text message.

After opting out, the user may receive a confirmation message indicating that SMS messaging has been stopped.

Help

For SMS messaging assistance, reply HELP to a FarmVista text message.

Mobile Information and Privacy

Mobile phone numbers, SMS consent, and SMS opt-in information will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

FarmVista may use service providers as necessary to deliver messages and operate FarmVista services.

Additional information about how FarmVista handles personal information is available in the FarmVista Privacy Policy.

Terms

Use of FarmVista SMS messaging is also subject to the FarmVista Terms and Conditions.
