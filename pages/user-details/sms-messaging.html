FarmVista SMS Messaging Program

FarmVista uses SMS text messaging to provide agricultural and operational communications to users who provide a mobile phone number and affirmatively consent to receive FarmVista text messages.

FarmVista SMS messages are operational communications. FarmVista does not use this SMS program for third-party marketing or promotional advertising.

Who May Opt In

FarmVista SMS messaging is intended for authorized FarmVista users, including employees and subcontracted agricultural drivers who may receive FarmVista work-related communications.

How Users Opt In

Users opt in through the authenticated User Details page inside the FarmVista application.

The user's mobile phone number is displayed in the FarmVista profile section.

Below the FarmVista Notifications section is a separate SMS Messaging section containing a FarmVista Text Messages toggle.

The FarmVista Text Messages toggle is OFF by default.

Providing or saving a mobile phone number does not automatically enroll the user in FarmVista SMS messaging.

The user must actively turn the SMS toggle ON to provide affirmative consent.

Before the user turns the toggle ON, the following disclosure is shown directly on the User Details page:

By turning this on, I agree to receive work-related SMS messages from FarmVista, including grain load dispatch notifications, grain ticket scan links, and authentication messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchasing goods or services.

The disclosure provides links to the FarmVista Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.

When the user actively turns the SMS toggle ON, FarmVista records the consent status, the mobile number associated with the consent, the consent source, and the consent timestamp.

Types of Messages

FarmVista SMS messages may include:

  • Grain load assignments and dispatch notifications.
  • Load numbers and crop information.
  • Buyer, customer, or delivery destination information.
  • Grain ticket submission or scanning instructions.
  • Secure links associated with assigned FarmVista work.
  • Authentication messages for approved FarmVista users.
  • Other FarmVista agricultural or operational notifications relevant to the user.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies.

The number of messages received depends on the number of FarmVista loads, assignments, jobs, alerts, authentication requests, or other operational activities associated with the user.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply depending on the user's wireless carrier and mobile plan.

Consent Is Optional

Consent to receive FarmVista SMS messages is optional and is not a condition of purchasing goods or services.

Providing a mobile phone number alone does not constitute consent to FarmVista SMS messaging.

Opt Out

Users may disable FarmVista SMS messaging by turning the FarmVista Text Messages toggle OFF on the User Details page.

Users may also stop receiving FarmVista SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to a FarmVista text message.

After opting out, the user may receive a confirmation message indicating that SMS messaging has been stopped.

Help

For FarmVista SMS messaging assistance, reply HELP to a FarmVista text message.

Mobile Information and Privacy

Mobile phone numbers, SMS consent, and SMS opt-in information will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

FarmVista may use service providers as necessary to deliver messages and operate FarmVista services.

Additional information about how FarmVista handles personal information is available in the FarmVista Privacy Policy.

Terms

Use of FarmVista SMS messaging is also subject to the FarmVista Terms and Conditions.
