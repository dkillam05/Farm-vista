The disclosure links to the FarmVista Terms and Conditions and FarmVista Privacy Policy.

When the user turns the SMS toggle ON, FarmVista records the user's SMS consent status and consent timestamp.

A publicly accessible example of the consent screen is available on the FarmVista SMS Consent Example page.
